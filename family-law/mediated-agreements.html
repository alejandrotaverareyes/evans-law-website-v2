Mediated agreements

Mediation is integral to the divorce process, whether a case is contested from the start or reaches agreements early. Some couples use a single mediator who represents both parties; that mediator explains the likely court outcome and helps the parties agree on division of assets and debts, the character and valuation of property, and custody and visitation.

Other cases begin contested. Our goal is to work proactively with the opposing party — represented or not — to narrow the issues for the court. California courts build this in: parties must meet and confer before hearings, and many hotly contested cases settle key issues without trial. Couples may also mediate before a private judge, with each side represented. For child custody, mediation is available through family court services and is often mandatory before contested custody can advance.

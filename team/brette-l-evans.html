Brette L. Evans

Partner · Bankruptcy Attorney · Family Law Attorney

Brette L. Evans brings over 25 years of ongoing, uninterrupted legal experience — practicing since 1995 — with a primary focus on bankruptcy and family law. Over her career she has also practiced intellectual property and civil litigation.

She works directly with clients, experts, families, and businesses to navigate legal hurdles, and seeks to push the limits of the law for the best possible outcomes — often working nights and weekends, and thriving on difficult cases.
